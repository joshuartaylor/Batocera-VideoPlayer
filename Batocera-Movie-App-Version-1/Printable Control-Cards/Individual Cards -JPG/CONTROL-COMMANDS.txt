BATOCERA MOVIE PLAYER V1 - ZAPAROO CONTROL COMMANDS

01  PLAY / PAUSE
    **control:toggle_pause

02  BACK 10 SECONDS
    **control:rewind

03  FORWARD 10 SECONDS
    **control:fast_forward

04  BACK 60 SECONDS
    **control:back_60

05  FORWARD 60 SECONDS
    **control:forward_60

06  VOLUME UP
    **control:volume_up

07  VOLUME DOWN
    **control:volume_down

08  MUTE / UNMUTE
    **control:mute

09  SUBTITLES ON / OFF
    **control:subtitles

10  NEXT SUBTITLE
    **control:next_subtitle

11  STOP / EXIT
    **control:stop

12  NEXT CHAPTER
    **control:next_chapter

13  PREVIOUS CHAPTER
    **control:previous_chapter

14  RESTART MOVIE
    **control:restart

15  FIT / FILL SCREEN
    **control:fit_screen

16  ZOOM PICTURE
    **control:zoom

17  MOVIE INFO
    **control:movie_info

18  PROGRESS / TIME
    **control:progress
